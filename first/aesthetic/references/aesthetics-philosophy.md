# Aesthetics philosophy

Aesthetics studies questions about aesthetic experience, judgement, value, art,
nature, beauty, and related modes of attention. Contemporary aesthetics is an
active university subject, not a fixed list of visual recipes.[^stanford-contemporary-aesthetics]

## Questions before claims

- What kind of attention should the artifact invite: ease, contemplation,
  tension, delight, estrangement, awe, intimacy, or something else?
- Is the judgement about sensory form, expressive meaning, cultural context,
  function, or their relation?
- Which response is intended, and which response is merely being assumed?
- What corpus and Preference Evidence support that intended response?

“Beauty” has multiple competing philosophical accounts; it should not be used
as though it names one measurable property.[^sep-beauty] Kant's account of
aesthetic judgement and sublimity is one influential framework, not a universal
style specification.[^sep-kant-aesthetics]

## Application to art direction

Name one dominant aesthetic question for a cohort, then describe the observable
formal relationships that test it. Beauty and sublimity are not mutually
exclusive switches, and neither is mandatory. A work may combine harmony with
friction or intimacy with scale. Report the tension instead of flattening it
into a label.

Philosophical framing is Rule Evidence. It never proves that the user likes a
direction; only element-level feedback supplies that Preference Evidence.
