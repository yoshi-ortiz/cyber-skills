# Review captures

A comp is judged from a picture of it, not from its markup, and the picture is
what a later reader needs to see what the rank meant. These are those pictures.

Sibling of `design/`, not a subdirectory of it by accident: the comps change
when a round is rerun, and a capture that moved with them would stop being
evidence of what was ranked.
