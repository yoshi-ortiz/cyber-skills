Round · construction language (craft), before character detail

Same figure, three craft treatments

The last round read as “toddler in MS Paint”—correctly, flat single-tone fills with a uniform outline and no shading is exactly that. Per construction doctrine, interior prop detail is also the wrong thing to test first: at delivery size the outline is the only mark left. So this strips every character back to one skeleton, one prop (glasses), and tests only fill/contour/shadow treatment. Judge the bottom row.

A · Flat & bold

What shipped so far: solid single-tone fills, uniform 6px contour, no shading, no shadow. The control — almost certainly the thing that reads as MS Paint.

at room size

B · Shaded & soft

Same skeleton, a radial highlight and gradient on every fill for volume, a thinner 3px contour, and a cast drop-shadow under the whole figure. Deterministic CSS gradients, not raster shading or a fetched asset.

at room size

C · Poster cutout

No outline anywhere. Identity carried by two flat, high-contrast shape colours and hard silhouette edges — a paper-cutout or riso-print register instead of a cartoon outline.

at room size
