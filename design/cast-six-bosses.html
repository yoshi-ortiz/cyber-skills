The cast, rebuilt

Rebuilt against what the reference images actually do, rather than what the manifest prose says about them. Corpus characters carry dot eyes, legs that stand on the floor, one bold silhouette shape each, and flat saturated fills under a heavy black contour. The previous rounds had none of those, which is what read as unfinished. Every colour here is measured from moodboards/. Judge the bottom row first.
