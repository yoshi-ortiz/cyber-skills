Before placing any architecture, establish one single, continuous yellow ribbon road shaped exactly like a horizontal infinity symbol: `∞`.

This is one closed road, not four spokes and not an X-shaped intersection with dead ends.

The road must contain:

- A complete rounded left outer loop.
- A complete rounded right outer loop.
- Two diagonal sections crossing once at the exact center.
- One clean over-under crossover at the center so the road remains visually traceable.
- No gaps behind rooms.
- No branches.
- No terminals.
- No disconnected segments.
- No duplicated roads.
- No ordinary four-way intersection.

The left outer curve must visibly connect `/first` to `/build`.\
The right outer curve must visibly connect `/land` to `/check`.

The road follows one uninterrupted clockwise workflow:

`/first` → down the left outer curve → `/build` → diagonally through the center → `/land` → down the right outer curve → `/check` → diagonally through the center → `/first`

Place repeated white directional arrows along the yellow road. Every arrow must point in that same sequence. No arrow may point backward or contradict another arrow. Use at least one arrow on each outer curve and one arrow on each diagonal section. The entire infinity loop and its one-way direction must be understandable immediately from a thumbnail view.

Rooms sit beside the road and connect through short entrances, but must never interrupt, conceal, replace, or terminate the road.

Do not render `/kit` as saturated orange, terracotta, brown, or bright coral.\
