Clean 2.5D isometric editorial infographic, cartoon-round: every room block is squat and low-walled, never a tall cube, with soft rounded corners and edges on every outline. Crisp dark outlines with round joins (no sharp miters), flat vector fills, simple faces, warm off-white background. Cute witty magazine style, not photorealistic, not glossy 3D, not pixel art, not stock art.

How this corpus builds a figure, read off the references:
- Eyes are large white ovals with the same heavy dark outline as the body, about a fifth of head width each. Never small solid dots on a large figure.
- Hair is two stacked colour zones, an outer mass and an inner fringe, meeting the face on a shaped edge. Never one flat blob, and never a hard block sitting on the head like a hat.
- Limbs overlap the body with their own closed outline. They never butt against the body edge.
- A small circle in the face colour sits proud of the head silhouette at ear height.
- Mouths are open shapes filled white inside the outline, not a small solid arc.
- No figure stands square. Arms sit at different angles from each other, heads tilt, figures lean on and hang off the scenery.
- Each figure carries three or four flat colour zones: hair, top, legs and footwear all differ.
- One outline weight on every part, round caps and joins, about a fortieth of figure height.

Never draw figures in this register:
- Never realistic human proportion and never a faceless figure. Head is about half of total height, and every face carries eyes and a mouth.
