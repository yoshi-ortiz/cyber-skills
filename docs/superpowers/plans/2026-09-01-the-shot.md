## The Shot itself

One round. One bounded deliverable. One verdict.

**Input.** The bounded correction from the rejected baseline. Its exact words
are on record and say the round did not improve the graphics or the thumbnail,
and that the output was useless.

**Steps.**

1. Run `tokens_qa.py correction .audit/shots/20260901T025137Z-a7052318.json`
   and feed that bundle, not the whole record, into refine.
2. Change the hero composition and the thumbnail crop or scale. Card chrome
   and harness plumbing do not count. The eval must be able to tell the
   difference, and `deliver_review_images` already refuses a cohort whose
   renders come out byte-identical to what is published.
3. Render at the declared desktop viewport and the exact thumbnail
   dimensions. Inspect the rendered PNGs, never the source HTML. The last
   round failed partly because nobody looked at the artifact.
4. `deliver` records the candidate automatically. Compare it against the
   baseline with `tokens_qa.py compare`.
5. Present both images and the comparison. Attach the verdict verbatim.

**Exit.** A candidate carrying fresh hero and real thumbnail proof, sitting at
`pending`. Machine proof must not be able to promote it, and
`test_shot_eval.py` asserts that as a requirement rather than a hope.

**If the verdict is rejection or correction**, repeat this round only. The
control plane does not change. Containment is what the bounded bundle bought.

## Three things a human has to decide

**1. `main` does change, and the plan's rule depends on nobody releasing it.**
`first/aesthetic` publishes to `main`, so the proof machinery and the R-15
split both alter ten files in the main tree, plus the new seams. Nothing has
been released, so the rule holds today only because no main release has run.
Either accept that main carries proof-aware aesthetic before the Shot is
judged, or put `aesthetic` back in `ALPHA_SKILLS` until it is. This is a
product call about what a user of the published skill should get.

**2. `release.py` refuses on untracked strays.**
`TODOS.md` and a hackathon schedule block it. They can no longer reach a
published tree, so the check is now stricter than the risk. Either delete or
commit them, or loosen the dirty check to ignore untracked files. Loosening a
release safety gate was not mine to do unasked.

**3. B-027 needs a human.**
The fix deletes a directory outside this repository.

```bash
rm -rf ~/.agents/skills/aesthetic
ln -s ~/Development/cyber-skills/first/aesthetic ~/.agents/skills/aesthetic
```

Until it is run, `first/aesthetic` is the only writable copy, and edits made
through the installed path are lost on the next sync.

## After acceptance

promotion to `main` after an explicit L3 `accepted` with no hard veto.
